Ask About Finance
Frequently asked questions

Good to know

How can I know the final vehicle price?

Prices shown are indicative. Contact VK EV MOTORS to confirm the current offer and on-road amount.

Is finance available?

Bajaj Finance assistance is available. Approval and EMI depend on eligibility and the finance partner's assessment.

Does every model have the same licence and registration rules?

Rules depend on the vehicle's approved specifications and applicable regulations. Please confirm the exact model's requirements with our team before purchase.

What is the warranty?

All models come with a 1 year warranty on motor and controller. Lithium battery models have a 2 year battery warranty; graphene battery models (Neutron, Green Sunny, Green Sunny Mini) have a 1 year battery warranty. Exact terms are shared at purchase.

Can I book a test ride?

Yes. Contact us on WhatsApp or phone to coordinate a test ride.
